Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy describes how Moss & Meridian uses cookies and similar browser storage on https://moss-meridian.example. Cookies are small files or storage entries that help a website remember preferences, understand performance, and provide a smoother visit. Our cookie banner gives visitors the ability to accept or reject non-essential cookies, and the choice is stored locally in the browser for future visits.

Essential cookies or local storage entries may be used to remember consent status, support security, keep forms usable, and maintain basic site functionality. Analytics or experience cookies, when accepted, help us learn which pages attract interest, how visitors navigate between services, and where content can be clarified. We use this information in aggregate and do not use cookies to sell personal profiles.

You can change cookie preferences by clearing browser storage or adjusting browser settings. Blocking some storage may cause banners to reappear or reduce convenience, but the core content remains accessible. Third party tools used for hosting, measurement, or embedded content may set their own technologies according to their policies, and we choose providers with responsible data practices.

The cookie system on this site includes an accept button, a reject button, and localStorage support. For questions about cookies or preference management, contact grow@moss-meridian.example.
