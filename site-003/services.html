Living Site Assessment

Moss & Meridian approaches living site assessment with the discipline of a specialist studio and the patience of an owner who expects the work to last. Every recommendation is shaped by interviews, field observation, commercial intent, and a clear point of view about how people should feel when they encounter the brand. The result is practical rather than decorative: the existing land is read as a living system before design choices are made. This service is built for leaders who need clarity before committing to expensive production. Clients receive plain language strategy, carefully sequenced priorities, and visible evidence that each decision is connected to a measurable experience outcome.
