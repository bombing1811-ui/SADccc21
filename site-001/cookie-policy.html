Cookie choices

This website uses a small cookie preference system to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies. The choice is stored in your browser using localStorage so the banner does not appear on every page. Essential browser storage may be used to keep the site functional, remember your preference, protect forms from abuse, and understand basic performance. If optional analytics are enabled in the future, they will be used to learn which pages are useful, how visitors navigate, and where improvements are needed.

You can accept or reject cookies using the banner buttons. You may also clear localStorage or browser data at any time to reset the choice. Rejecting optional cookies will not prevent you from reading pages or contacting Kanso Current Advisory, though some future enhancements may be less personalized. We do not use cookies to sell personal information or follow you across unrelated websites. For privacy questions, contact hello@kansocurrent.example.

When we update this policy or related legal pages, the revised version will be posted here with the current language replacing prior text. Continued use of the website after an update means you accept the revised terms for future visits. Material project agreements, if any, remain governed by their separate signed documents.
