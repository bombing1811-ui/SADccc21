Cookie choices

This website uses a small cookie preference system to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies. The choice is stored in your browser using localStorage so the banner does not appear on every page. Essential browser storage may be used to keep the site functional, remember your preference, protect forms from abuse, and understand basic performance. If optional analytics are enabled in the future, they will be used to learn which pages are useful, how visitors navigate, and where improvements are needed.

You can accept or reject cookies using the banner buttons. You may also clear localStorage or browser data at any time to reset the choice. Rejecting optional cookies will not prevent you from reading pages or contacting Cipher Quill Review, though some future enhancements may be less personalized. We do not use cookies to sell personal information or follow you across unrelated websites. For privacy questions, contact desk@cipherquill.example.

We may update this page when website practices, service descriptions, or applicable requirements change. The version posted here applies to future website use.
