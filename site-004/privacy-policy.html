Privacy practices

Cipher Quill Review respects the privacy of visitors, prospective clients, collaborators, and newsletter readers. This website may collect information that you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organization, project notes, and any message submitted through a contact form. We use that information to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential engagements, provide requested updates, and maintain appropriate business records. We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device category, referring page, and approximate usage patterns through ordinary server logs or privacy-conscious analytics. We do not sell personal information, and we do not build advertising profiles from your visit.

Information is shared only with service providers who help us operate the website, manage communications, or deliver professional services, and only when they are expected to protect it responsibly. If an inquiry contains confidential, regulated, or sensitive information, please contact us first so we can agree on a suitable channel. We keep information only as long as needed for the purpose it was provided, legal obligations, or legitimate business continuity. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting desk@cipherquill.example. Because no online transmission is perfectly secure, please avoid sending secrets or highly sensitive materials through public forms.

We may update this page when website practices, service descriptions, or applicable requirements change. The version posted here applies to future website use. Any confidentiality, privilege, incident support, or advisory obligations for a client matter must be set out in a separate written agreement before work begins.
