Privacy practices

PulseForge Performance Kitchen respects the privacy of visitors, prospective clients, collaborators, and newsletter readers. This website may collect information that you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organization, project notes, and any message submitted through a contact form. We use that information to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential engagements, provide requested updates, and maintain appropriate business records. We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device category, referring page, and approximate usage patterns through ordinary server logs or privacy-conscious analytics. We do not sell personal information, and we do not build advertising profiles from your visit.

Information is shared only with service providers who help us operate the website, manage communications, or deliver professional services, and only when they are expected to protect it responsibly. If an inquiry contains confidential, regulated, or sensitive information, please contact us first so we can agree on a suitable channel. We keep information only as long as needed for the purpose it was provided, legal obligations, or legitimate business continuity. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting team@pulseforge.example. Because no online transmission is perfectly secure, please avoid sending secrets or highly sensitive materials through public forms.

Policy updates may be made as the website, services, or applicable practices change. The current version posted on this page controls future visits. Professional service agreements, nutrition responsibilities, confidentiality terms, and project-specific obligations are handled separately in written agreements approved by the client and PulseForge.
